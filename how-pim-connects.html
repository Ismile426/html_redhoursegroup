How F&O connects to Pimcore

For Red Horse. 23 Sep 2026. What the connection does, without the technical payload. The build reference for developers is a separate page. The exact JSON is on the payload page.

The connection

Pimcore does not look inside F&O. A small program on the F&O side pushes a batch of data into Pimcore. Pimcore stores it as catalogue folders and products. People in Pimcore then add the pictures, the Danish and Swedish shop text, and the catalogue icons.

In one sentence

F&O says what the product is. Pimcore holds it in a fixed shape and adds the selling text and the pictures.

The test address is on the Red Horse network only. Azure cannot reach it yet. Every push needs a key. There is no login session.

The order that is live

Each box is one push. Run them in this order on a first load. The next box needs what the earlier boxes created in Pimcore. Sending the same thing again updates what is already there. It does not make a second copy. Technical detail and “needs first” table: payload page → Order & rules.

1. CompaniesWhich F&O companies exist
2. Colours and sizesThe lists a variant can use
3. FoldersType, family, series, range, drawing
4. What a fact isHead, diameter, length — the definition
5. ProductsThe product and the item you can order
6. The factsThe head, the material, the length — needs products
7. Names in F&O languagesEnglish Canada is the main name — needs products; can follow step 6

Step 4 (definitions) must come after folders (step 3). Step 5 (products) must come after folders and colour/size lists (steps 2–3). Steps 6 and 7 both need step 5.

What a screw looks like

One screw is seven levels. The top five are folders. The last two are the product and the thing a customer orders.

TypeScrews, or washers.
FamilyThe brand, such as CREO.
SeriesHead type lives here.
RangeMaterial, coating, and corrosion. This is the category.
DrawingDiameter and drill cap.
ProductOne product, many lengths. Length lives here.
Item you orderColour, size, and the other choices.
What a person sees

After the folders, the tree exists but the products are empty. After the product push, the product and the orderable item exist, still without a head type or a length. After the facts, the series shows the head, the range shows material and corrosion category, the drawing shows the diameter, and the product shows the length. The orderable item does not get its own head type. It shows the head from the series.

What F&O does not send

  • The catalogue icon on a material (the A2 badge) and on a corrosion category (the C4 badge). F&O sends the code. A person uploads the picture.
  • Danish and Swedish shop title and shop description.
  • Technical pictures, pack labels, and certificates.

Not connected yet

  • Which shop sells the item. The shop stays as it is until that push exists.
  • Which washer is sold with a style-W screw. That number is accepted and not used until F&O confirms it is the washer’s own item number.
